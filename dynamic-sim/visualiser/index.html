How the model works

  • Each ball's id, size and arrival time are hashed. With two choices the hash names two different bins, and the ball joins the one with less backlog. Ties go to the first bin checked.
  • A bin with efficiency e processes a ball of size s in s/e units of time, first come first served. Tube height measures work: a size-1 ball is one unit tall.
  • A ball's wait runs from its arrival until its processing starts, and counts toward the averages when the ball finishes.
  • A retired bin takes no new balls. It finishes its queue and is then removed. The last bin taking balls cannot be retired.
  • The theory line is the Pollaczek–Khinchine mean wait for one choice. Hashing splits a Poisson stream evenly, so each bin behaves as an M/G/1 queue with E[S²] = eσ².
